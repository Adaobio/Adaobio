## How I evaluate AI systems

I do not judge an AI workflow only by the quality of a demo.

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| **Business value** | What customer or revenue problem does this solve? |
| **Quality** | How are correctness, usefulness, and failure measured? |
| **Economics** | What does the workflow cost, and what outcome justifies it? |
| **Adoption** | Who must change behavior, and how will usage be sustained? |
| **Trust** | Where are privacy, security, human oversight, and escalation required? |

I distinguish between **observed results**, **measured prototype results**, **modeled outcomes**, and **synthetic demonstrations**. I do not present forecasts or synthetic data as real customer impact.
